Mountains are massive landforms that rise prominently above their surroundings, typically featuring steep slopes, exposed bedrock, and a peak or summit. They are Earth's primary water towers and biodiversity strongholds, shaping global weather systems and sustaining over half of the world's population with fresh water.

Fold Mountains: Formed when tectonic plates collide, buckling and folding the Earth's crust (e.g., the Himalayas).
